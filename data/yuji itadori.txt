Yuji Itadori possesses freakish baseline superhuman strength, speed, and durability, allowing him to throw cars, sprint at speeds matching Olympic vehicles, and shatter concrete walls even without using cursed energy. He is a highly skilled martial artist who excels at close-quarters combat. When he infuses his physical blows with his supernatural power, he can use the Divergent Fist, a technique where his physical punch hits first and a delayed wave of cursed energy explodes a split second later. He is also a master of the Black Flash, a legendary spatial distortion strike that multiplies physical power by 2.5 times and holds the record for unleashing the most consecutive strikes in history.
Because he hosted the King of Curses and trained with high-level allies, Yuji can naturally perceive and strike the outer contours of a person's soul, bypassing standard physical armor. He later masters the Reverse Cursed Technique to heal severed limbs and fatal wounds instantly, alongside a defensive Simple Domain to protect himself from absolute enemy attacks. By consuming the remaining Death Painting Wombs, Yuji unlocks Blood Manipulation, which lets him fire pressurized blood like high-velocity lasers through Piercing Blood or create shrapnel bursts with Supernova, while safely converting his own energy into blood to prevent ever bleeding out.
In his final evolution, Sukuna's innate technique engraves itself onto Yuji's body as Shrine, allowing him to unleash precision slashing attacks called Cleave and Dismantle. He modifies these into Soul Dismantles, which specifically slice the boundaries between two souls to tear possessing entities away from their hosts. Yuji completes his growth by awakening his own Domain Expansion, a barrier that takes the form of his nostalgic, snowy childhood hometown, where he can project memories directly into an opponent's soul and ensure a definitive end to the fight.

Megumi Fushiguro is an exceptionally skilled tactical jujutsu sorcerer who possesses peak human physical traits, high intellectual prowess, and advanced weapon proficiency. Unlike most sorcerers who focus purely on brawling, Megumi acts as a battlefield strategist. He utilizes a rare, instinctual martial arts style that allows him to seamlessly integrate hand-to-hand combat with his shadow manipulation, keeping opponents off balance by constantly striking from their blind spots.
His primary hereditary power is the Ten Shadows Technique, which grants him the ability to summon up to ten distinct supernatural Shikigami creatures by using his hands to cast shadow puppets. These familiars include the Divine Dogs for tracking and tracking-based combat, Nue for aerial electrical attacks, Toad for restraining targets, and Max Elephant for crushing foes with high-pressure water streams. If a summoned Shikigami is permanently destroyed, its power and abilities are inherited by the surviving beasts, making his overall arsenal stronger over time.
Megumi’s ultimate summon is the Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga, the most powerful and untamable entity in the history of his technique. Mahoraga possesses the unmatched ability to adapt to any and all phenomena, meaning once it survives an opponent's specific attack, it instantly develops a flawless countermeasure to negate it. Megumi can also drop into his shadow at will to store items or travel stealthily, and he can manifest an incomplete Domain Expansion called Chimera Shadow Garden, which floods the entire battlefield in a sea of liquid shadows to create endless duplicates of his Shikigami.

Satoru Gojo is widely recognized as the strongest jujutsu sorcerer of the modern era, possessing unparalleled physical strength, speed, and martial arts mastery that allow him to outmaneuver multiple special-grade opponents simultaneously. His combat style is near-flawless because he possesses the Limitless technique, which gives him absolute spatial control over infinity. He is also born with the Six Eyes, a legendary ocular trait that processes visual and supernatural information at an atomic level, allowing him to optimize his cursed energy consumption to nearly zero so he never runs out of power in a fight.
His primary defense is the Infinity, a passive barrier that slows down any incoming attack or object infinitely before it can ever touch his body, rendering him effectively untouchable. For offense, Gojo manipulates space to create devastating kinetic phenomena. Blue pulls matter together like a high-powered gravitational black hole, while Red uses positive energy to repel matter with a massive, explosive force. By combining these two forces, he unleashes Hollow Purple, a massive destructive sphere that obliterates everything in its path by ripping through the fabric of reality itself.
Gojo is a master of advanced barrier arts, utilizing the Reverse Cursed Technique to instantly heal severe physical injuries and continuously refresh his own brain from exhaustion. His ultimate peak ability is his Domain Expansion, known as Unlimited Void. When activated, this barrier traps targets inside a metaphysical space that forces an infinite stream of raw information and sensory data directly into their brains, completely paralyzing their minds and leaving them entirely helpless to his finishing blows.
